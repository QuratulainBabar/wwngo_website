01What is WWNGO

International delivery through real journeys

Not a courier franchise — a marketplace built around people already traveling.

WWNGO lets senders post parcel requests along international routes. Travelers who are already flying or driving can carry items within agreed limits, earn compensation, and hand off securely to receivers. Payments are held in escrow until delivery is confirmed in the app.

Real trips, agreed limits

Travelers flying or driving carry parcels within terms agreed in the app.

02Overview

Three roles, one connected flow

Every delivery follows the same transparent path from request to release.

  1. 01

    Sender

    Sender posts a request

    Describe the parcel, route, and timing. WWNGO helps match you with a verified traveler heading your way.

  2. 02

    Traveler

    Traveler accepts and carries

    Funds move to escrow via Stripe. The traveler picks up the item, updates tracking, and travels with your parcel.

  3. 03

    Receiver

    Receiver confirms handoff

    NFC or in-app confirmation completes delivery. Escrow releases payment to the traveler after successful handoff.

07FAQ

Common questions

View all FAQ
Can I book a delivery on this website?

No. WWNGO bookings, payments, and handoffs happen in the mobile app. This site explains how the marketplace works.

How is my payment protected?

Customer funds move to escrow via Stripe when a traveler accepts, and are released to the traveler only after a successful, confirmed handoff.

Who can become a traveler?

People already flying or driving on international routes can carry parcels after completing KYC identity verification and agreeing to the marketplace rules.
